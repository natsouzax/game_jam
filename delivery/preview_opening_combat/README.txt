TOGETHER - local preview
Open Play.cmd on Windows. The first screen shows Lusitanos Games, then the supplied story video, then the menu.
P1: A/D move, W jump, E build or break. P2: Left/Right move, Up jump, Enter build or break.
On an existing player block, press the action twice within 0.5 seconds to break it. The second press refunds one material.
Enemy kills award one material to the finishing player. Meteors break player blocks.
This preview passed startup playback, focused combat/build checks, and short regression checks. The complete automated climb replay still needs repair and a human playtest.
