TOGETHER
Theme: A better world

A local two-player co-op platform game with an optional solo mode. Choose a survivor, collect materials falling from above, place blocks, climb, recover from hazards, and reach the top. The story unfolds through images and animation. The title is the only text displayed in the game.

Windows 64-bit build. P1 or solo: A/D to move, W to jump, E to build. Co-op P2: Left/Right, Up, Enter. The external README explains the menu and audio settings. Music and sound are included; the settings screen can mute music and adjust its volume.

Team: Natan, Guilherme, Luiz, Emmanuel, Vanessa, and Vitally. The project was developed during the jam. Some artwork was generated with AI; details are in ART_AND_SOURCES.md. The release includes the Windows build and completed LDMT Plan/Final workbooks. No upload has been made.
